Discover accurate and efficient PINN solvers, autonomously

PINNSeek, a knowledge-centric agent system for the autonomous discovery of physics-informed neural network (PINN) solvers, pairs two knowledge bases with a coding agent that has agency over their use. The literature base distills human-verified knowledge from the PINN literature, and the experience base accumulates task-specific knowledge during discovery.

    The problem

    Find the PINN solver with the lowest expected error for the task, under a total GPU budget B.

    A candidate solver, a program that defines a training function and a prediction function.
    The admissible set of the task, the programs that solve the problem as a PINN with derivatives by automatic differentiation, keep the interface of the standard PINN and read no data beyond the public files.
    The seed of the training, and the expectation over seeds.
    The relative L2 error at the scoring points, against a reference solution that the agent never reads.

    Every evaluation trains a candidate on one GPU for the training time τ of the problem, 60 to 480 s. A budget B of 5 to 20 GPU hours affords evaluations in the order of 102.

    A solver is a whole program, so the design space includes the network, the loss, the sampling, the optimizer, the training schedule and the way the constraints are imposed.

    Knowledge

    A literature base of 130 human-verified notes, and an experience base that accumulates retrospective evidence.

    The literature base, one note per paper on PINN methods from 2019 to 2026. A note gives the mechanism, the failure of training it addresses, an implementation in JAX and the reported results.
    The experience base after round r, the summaries of all rounds so far.
    The direction, the parent from which round r starts and the reasons for its line of attack.
    The table of every evaluation of the round, with the change of its candidate, its error and its number of steps.
    The findings, what works, what fails and why, and what later rounds should drop or pursue.

    Each round starts a new agent that inherits nothing but the files of the run, so the experience base is the memory of the discovery. Its errors come only from the evaluation tool, and its summaries cite the notes by their identifiers.

    Agency

    The coding agent chooses the parent, the notes it reads, what each candidate changes and how it is evaluated.

    The coding agent of the round, which follows the charter χ.
    The task, the literature base and the experience base before the round.
    The candidate programs and the ledger of evaluation records of the earlier rounds.
    The GPU budget of the round.
    What the round returns, its candidates, their evaluation records and its summary.

    PINNSeek fixes the inputs, the charter, the budget and the order of the rounds. The policy of the agent decides everything else, the parent, which may be any earlier candidate, the notes it reads, what each candidate changes, from one hyperparameter to the whole solver, and the number, order and seeds of the evaluations.

    A fixed search strategy, such as FunSearch, AlphaEvolve or ERA, instead selects the parent by a rule set in advance and fills a fixed prompt template.

    Superiority · Accuracy · Efficiency

    More accurate than PINN solvers designed by human experts

    The discovered solvers and Scale-PINN, APTS, SPINN and DSGNAR are each trained with five seeds on one NVIDIA RTX A6000 GPU, for the same time per problem, 60 to 480 s.

    Generalization

    Solvers carry over to new cases without redesign

    Applied to 30 new cases of LDC, BFS, NACA, KF and KS, with other Reynolds numbers, angles of attack, initial conditions and domain lengths and a second airfoil, the solvers retrained from scratch reach a mean error below 0.02 on 23 cases and below 0.05 on 27. Scale-PINN, trained for the same time, has a higher error on 28 of the 30 (one-sided Mann–Whitney test, p ≤ 0.05).

    One more discovery round on a new case lowers the error on 15 of the 30 cases.

    Applications

    Atmospheric transport and fluid dynamics

    Smoke transport from the 2023 wildfires in Quebec with reanalysis winds and fire emissions, the vortex street behind a cylinder in the DFG 2D-3 benchmark, and the three-dimensional flow over the ONERA M6 wing.

    Inside a discovery

    The agent builds on literature knowledge and on its own experience

    Across three runs each on LDC and KS, the largest advances come when a technique enters the solver. Of the 44 components of the six final solvers, 17 are taken from the literature base unchanged, 13 are adapted from it and 14 do not come from it.

    The experience base accumulates what each round finds, and the agent revises it as the solver changes.

    Independent agents reach similar accuracy along different paths.

    Ablations

    Knowledge and agency drive the accuracy

    Under the same agent model, task and GPU budget, runs without the literature base end at a geometric mean error of 0.20 on LDC, against 0.015 for PINNSeek. A single round with the whole budget ends at 0.17, the tree search of ERA at 0.66 and Claude Code with web search at 0.44.
